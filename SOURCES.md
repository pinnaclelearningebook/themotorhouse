# SOURCES.md

Every external factual claim made on the site, where it appears, and where it came from.

**Why this file exists.** The brand's entire position is that we do not overstate things. If the ASA, a journalist or a seller queries a claim, the answer should take one minute to find, not a re-research session. Section 12 of CLAUDE.md requires every claim to be substantiable — this is how we hold to that.

**Rules.**

- Add a row here the moment you write a sourced claim. No claim ships without one.
- If you cannot source it, do not write it. Use `<AwaitingInfo />` instead.
- Claims about our own business (collection area, payment timing, offer validity) are commitments, not external facts. They belong in PENDING-INFO.md until confirmed, not here.
- Re-check dated figures periodically. Anything with a year in it will age.
